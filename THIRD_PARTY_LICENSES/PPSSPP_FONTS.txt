PPSSPP Replacement PGF Fonts
============================

This is an evidence packet, not a license grant. Issue #99 remains open:
the exact source-file/release and complete redistribution notice for each
checked-in replacement PGF have not been proved. Do not describe the four
PGFs as GPL merely because PPSSPP's program is GPL-2.0-or-later, and do not
describe the compatibility names in their headers as proof of Sony or
Fontworks outlines.

----------------------------------------

font_type at offset 0x75 (64 bytes each, as defined by the public pgf.h in
tpunix/pgftool). The current values and the strongest conclusion supported by
the upstream history are:

These are source-family findings, not legal conclusions. The current
camouflage commit also changed bold/italic properties, so it must not be
treated as a header-only edit or as proof that every outline byte is
unchanged.

Generator and metadata mechanism
---------------------------------

The public tpunix/pgftool repository was independently cloned at
a9b3114bf6123c0bb1840255ed3129396085013b. Its original font-conversion
commit is edce5c60096cba9d7e7dae71ea2d68d6913b9697
(2013-02-28, "My PGF font tools."). In ttfont.c, face_to_pgf() copies
FreeType face->family_name into the PGF font_name field and
face->style_name into font_type; the current tree adds a null-family fallback
to "Dummy Font". This mechanically explains why the PGF header names are
useful evidence of the metadata supplied to the converter at generation time.
The pgftool repository has no discovered license/notice file, so the tool
history is not a font license grant.

PPSSPP history and camouflage
-----------------------------

The paths begin in commit 1429d0206ac152fe4c77936f57016c9279467b12
(2013-02-27, "Add JPCSP's free font files") and move under assets/ in
8d1ece05ca5d7de2e1926b8c7469a5d5f616c6b8. Nassau-tk's replacement work is
visible in the following public commits/issues:

- jpn0: 77c1e96588718b83527fac9dfe395c2e5eddf4f7 (2020-10-09) says the
  PGF was generated by Nassau and points to issue #13510; f68e0fecd4f52b9836b8aa031248f2a1d3489bce
  (2020-10-26) applies manual JIS fixes.
- jpn0 Source Han line: f1a3ba7f5669204a964e4a49c0354d8ab64e6dbc and
  388ac3c9aeb69c86e269eb1ad16fb8d1ef2a5fcd (2020-11-23) record
  Source Han Sans in the header; 0c243ee13909a1256de4be2b9d374aa6f7b46d38
  is the 2020-12-08 all-font replacement batch.
- kr0: 75bdb5ff1723bd39574baf11336c5c8a3173deec (2020-11-30) records
  Source Han Sans for Nassau's latest Korean font.
- ltn0/ltn8: 06b0e33839b4260caa7f950df7e7a19961395e70, 737e0d55d4a06cb026ec453010e1860ecc23df97,
  and b5e23007700fc026c4fddea45e87f345d2a41d32 record the Ume Hy Gothic
  even-family revisions. Issue #13589 separately identifies odd ltn1/3/5/7
  as Ume-Font-Mincho.
- ltn8-15: issue #13718 records conversion with tpunix pgftool and later
  PGF-level metric edits, but does not name an exact source TTF or release.
- camouflage: dc34bea8d4dee77629da3cbaedc83fc8c05af159
  (parent 4595fcd83b796a683ab3abb5f86c42de51e4ea8c, 2021-01-20) is titled
  "PGF Fixed Bold & Italic property and camouflage the Font name." It
  changes 18 PGFs. The four target names change from Source Han Sans or
  Ume Hy Gothic to FTT-NewRodin Pro DB, AsiaKNHH-SONY-uni, or
  FTT-NewRodin Pro Latin while the target styles remain Regular.

Historical source-license evidence
-----------------------------------

Source Han Sans:

- Adobe's public Source Han Sans source tree at tags 2.001 and 2.002 was
  checked locally. The 2.001 LICENSE.txt notice begins "Copyright 2014-2019
  Adobe ... Reserved Font Name 'Source'" and states the SIL Open Font License,
  Version 1.1. The 2.002 LICENSE.txt as published begins "Copyright -2020
  Adobe ... Reserved Font Name 'Source'" and likewise states OFL 1.1; the
  unusual "-2020" spelling is retained here as a transcription of that
  historical file, not normalized.
- PPSSPP's public issues and headers identify the family but do not name the
  exact TTF filename, Adobe release tag, or subset used by Nassau. 2.001 and
  2.002 are contemporaneous public reference releases, not a claim about the
  exact input. The exact source release/file remains UNKNOWN.

UmeFont:

- The Debian 465-1 copyright record identifies upstream Umefont, Wataru
  Horai, and the SourceForge/OSDN release project. It lists the Gothic,
  P-Gothic, UI-Gothic, Mincho, and P-Mincho families and records the UmeFont
  permission to use, copy, distribute, and modify commercially or
  noncommercially, with no warranty. Ubuntu's historical package record
  preserves the same upstream years and terms.
- Fedora's font review page explicitly notes that Ume has its own license and
  required legal review. This is a warning against treating package metadata
  as blanket legal clearance.
- Nassau's issue #13510 comment names ume-pgs5.ttf for the October jpn0
  conversion and quotes the same Ume permission. The current jpn0 blob is
  from a later Source Han line, so that filename cannot be assigned to the
  current blob. The exact Ume file/release used for the current ltn0/ltn8
  line is likewise UNKNOWN.

Publication rule
----------------

Until #99 is closed for a specific PGF, keep that binary out of the
public-safe profile or require a properly licensed user-supplied replacement.
Do not change SPDX text or call this packet legal clearance. PPSSPP's
GPL-2.0-or-later program license is not a grant for these data files, and the
repository-level GPL-3.0-or-later declaration is not a substitute for this
component review.
